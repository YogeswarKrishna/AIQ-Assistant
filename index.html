Intelligence that adapts to the question.

AIQ is a private, offline-first AI assistant that dynamically chooses the smallest level of intelligence needed — retrieves your local knowledge, reasons when necessary, and verifies the answer before you trust it.

Why AIQ?

Not just an offline chatbot — an adaptive local AI system.

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Your questions and local knowledge can remain on your device during core offline Q&A.

📴 OFFLINE FIRST

Core Q&A continues without an internet connection when the required models, indexes and knowledge are available locally.

⚖ ADAPTIVE COMPUTATION

Simple questions don't need the largest model.

🔎 EVIDENCE GROUNDED

Answers can be checked against the local evidence used to generate them.

Not every question needs the same amount of intelligence.

LEVEL 1 · ROUTER
SLM

LEVEL 2 · QUICK

AIQ spends more computation only when the question requires it.

How it works

01 UNDERSTAND

AIQ analyzes intent, topic and complexity.

02 ROUTE

The adaptive router selects the appropriate intelligence level.

03 RETRIEVE

Relevant local knowledge is retrieved.

04 REASON

The selected model generates the response.

05 VERIFY

The response is checked against evidence.

06 ANSWER

The user receives the grounded response and evidence.

Your knowledge stays yours.

AIQ's core Q&A workflow can operate locally when the required models, indexes, documents and inference components are installed on the device.
